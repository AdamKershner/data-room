---

## 1. Why this project exists

Kahana grows primarily through organic Clubs, library boarding, and creator outreach. Many early-stage companies also grow by forming **distribution channels with partners** — and sometimes by sharing revenue when a partner brings attributed demand.

Before we negotiate deals or build affiliate tooling, we need a clear internal map of:

- What **types** of distribution partnerships Kahana could have
- How each type compares (what the partner gives vs what Kahana gives)
- Whether economics are **attention-only** (links, embeds, co-marketing) or **revenue-split** (when attribution justifies it)
- How this fits our posture: **use Kahana alongside peers**, not rip-and-replace

This charter scopes a research + taxonomy deliverable Rohan can complete quickly. It is **not** a mandate to close partnerships in this sprint.

---

## 2. Purpose (one sentence)

Map and list all meaningful **distribution partnership types** Kahana could pursue, with compare/contrast notes and recommended deal shapes, so the team can brief prospects and prioritize BD.

---

## 3. Success criteria

The project is done when Adam (sponsor) can open the deliverable and immediately see:

1. A **taxonomy** of partnership types (categories + subtypes), not a random company list  
2. For each type: what they distribute, what Kahana offers, example partners, and suggested economics (none / affiliate / rev share / reseller)  
3. Clear **guardrails** (e.g. do not sell Aura ranking; prefer Club-ready demand)  
4. A short **priority shortlist** (top 5 types to explore next) with rationale  
5. Alignment with existing materials: `/kahana` synergy cards and `/battlecards` (tandem / use-both framing)

**Not success criteria for this sprint:** signed LOIs, live affiliate codes, or product integrations.

---

## 4. Scope

### In scope

- Desktop research + synthesis from existing data room materials (synergy cards, battlecards, Fragment Map / GTM, library narrative)
- Taxonomy of **partnership types** (channels), with 2–4 example companies per type where helpful
- For each type: compare/contrast with Kahana; “using both”; when a revenue split makes sense vs free distribution
- Rough deal-shape guidance tied to Kahana economics today (Growth SaaS; ~5% hub take rate) — directional, not legal terms
- One-pager summary + fuller working doc (Google Doc or markdown in data room)
- Optional: short table Adam can paste into investor/team updates

### Out of scope

- Negotiating or signing real partner agreements  
- Building affiliate software, tracking links, or payout systems  
- Changing product pricing or take rate  
- Cold outreach campaigns (may be a follow-on project)  
- Publishing house catalog licensing (separate legal/B2B track)  
- Paid ads as a “partnership”

## 7. Working principles (from sponsor)

1. **Distribution ≠ automatic revenue share.** Share attention freely; share revenue when attribution is clear and incremental.  
2. **Tandem first.** Partners should feel complementary (Teachable, YouTube, Discord, Beacons, etc.).  
3. **Protect Aura.** Never sell ranking or guaranteed top placement as “paid Aura.” Featured placement (editorial) is different — label it clearly.  
4. **Prefer Club-ready demand** over raw clicks.  
5. **Keep economics inside Kahana’s model** — don’t promise partners a cut of GMV that leaves creators or Kahana underwater (think share of *our* take or SaaS, not 20% of creator GMV).  
6. **Living draft.** Market research will refine this; v1 is a map for conversation, not final BD policy.

## 11. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| List becomes a flat company dump instead of types | Force taxonomy first; companies only as examples |
| Over-indexing on rev share before product-market fit | Default economics = free distribution; splits only with attribution story |
| Conflict with “use both” brand | Reuse battlecard/synergy language; no “replace X” framing |
| Scope creep into live outreach | Out of scope — capture follow-ons in a parking lot |
